# CS Consolidation Engine – Master Index

## Welcome to Your Long-Term Mastery System

This is not a course. This is a **continuous consolidation loop** designed to rebuild and deepen your Computer Science foundations across all major domains.

---

## 🎯 System Philosophy

1. **Precise Theory**: Formal definitions, mechanisms, proofs
2. **Stress Testing**: Conceptual questions that expose gaps
3. **Applied Problems**: Multi-part reasoning challenges
4. **Rigorous Feedback**: Critique on logic, edge cases, assumptions, complexity
5. **Cross-Domain Integration**: Explicit links between areas
6. **Weakness Tracking**: Systematic identification of recurring patterns
7. **Progressive Difficulty**: Escalates gradually over time

---

## 📚 The 7 Domains (Cycle Continuously)

Your sessions cycle through these domains:

1. **[[Computation & Memory]]** – Cache hierarchies, memory models, cost analysis

## 🗓️ Session Structure (All Sessions Follow This)

### Phase 1: Clean Theory
- **Definitions** (formal, minimal)
- **Core Mechanism** (how it works)
- **Mental Model** (intuition)
- **Edge Cases** (where theory breaks)
- **Common Mistakes** (what people get wrong)
- **Code Snippets** (if applicable)

### Phase 2: Conceptual Stress Questions (3 questions)
- **Wait for your full answer**
- No hints given
- Test understanding, not memorization

### Phase 3: Applied Problem (Multi-part)
- **Wait for your attempt**
- Combines multiple concepts
- Requires quantitative reasoning

### Phase 4: Feedback & Weakness Log
- **After you respond**
- Critique: logic, rigor, edge cases, complexity
- Update weakness log
- Suggest reinforcement links

## 📋 How to Study

### Your Workflow:

1. **Open a session** (start with Session 1)
2. **Read Phase 1** carefully (theory)
3. **Answer Phase 2 questions** (conceptual)
   - Write full answers in the session file or a separate response
   - Show your reasoning
4. **Solve Phase 3 problem** (applied)
   - Work through all parts
   - State assumptions explicitly
5. **Request feedback** from me
6. **Review critique** and update weakness log
7. **Move to next session** (cycle through domains)

### Pacing Recommendations:

- **1 session every 2-3 days** for deep engagement
- **Don't rush** – precision over speed
- **Revisit weak areas** using cross-links
- **Track patterns** in your weakness log

## 🎓 Feedback Philosophy

I will critique based on:

1. **Logical Rigor**: Are your arguments sound?
2. **Missing Assumptions**: Did you state all prerequisites?
3. **Edge Cases**: Did you consider boundary conditions?
4. **Complexity Analysis**: Did you compute actual costs?
5. **Tradeoffs**: Did you analyze multiple approaches?
6. **Abstraction Clarity**: Is your reasoning at the right level?

**Expect**: Pointed feedback, not praise. The goal is precision.
