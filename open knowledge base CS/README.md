## 🗺️ The 7 Domains (All Cycles)

Each cycle covers these domains with increasing sophistication:

1. **[[Computation & Memory]]** – Cache, memory models, performance
2. **[[Algorithms & Complexity]]** – Analysis, data structures, optimization
3. **[[Discrete Math & Probability]]** – Formal methods, randomization
4. **[[Databases & Concurrency]]** – Transactions, consistency, isolation
5. **[[Networks & Distributed Systems]]** – Consensus, replication, CAP
6. **[[ML & Optimization]]** – Convexity, gradient methods, convergence
7. **[[Systems Design Integration]]** – Architecture, bottlenecks, tradeoffs
